Smoke filter: three smoky Sentinel-2 scenes over the catchment on 3 August 2025 passed the cloud mask and made 2025 look like forest loss. Every year's composite now drops pixels whose aerosol optical thickness is above 0.4; 2025 keeps a clear view of 99.3% of the catchment.

Agreement with MODIS land cover

Same years, same bounding box, same denominator (percent of land). On average the model is — points above MODIS for forest, — for cropland and — for urban. The model stays close to the 2020 federal map it learned from; MODIS is far below that map too, so the gap comes mostly from how the two products define classes (MODIS puts much mixed farm and woodlot land in savanna and mosaic classes) and its 500 m pixels. It is reported here, not tuned away.

How it was built
  • Model: Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M (model card: jointly developed by IBM, NASA and Jülich Supercomputing Centre; Apache-2.0) fine-tuned for six land-cover classes with TerraTorch. Registered in MLflow as land-cover-ca: version 1 (v1), 2 (v2 class-weighted, best) and 3 (v2). Runs are in the MLflow experiment lakepulse-land-cover. The best model's weights, card and checksums are on Hugging Face: anjanid/land-cover-ca.
  • Imagery: Sentinel-2 L2A (Earth Search), summer median composites at 30 m, six bands, clouds, shadow and snow masked. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2017 to 2025.
  • Labels: 2020 Land Cover of Canada, 30 m, merged to six classes. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Canada.
  • Split by area, never by pixel: held-out test block (Holland River and the Cook's Bay shore, 541.3 km², 43.2 km of shoreline), validation in the Pefferlaw River watershed, training on the rest of the 13 watersheds around the lake plus 22 neighbouring ones. Chips within 6.72 km of a test chip were dropped.
  • v2 training data: 636 chips from the 2019, 2020 and 2021 summers against the 2020 labels. v1 used 86 chips from 2020 only and did not beat gradient boosting.
  • Strip: land and water within 30 m of the Lake Simcoe shoreline or an Ontario Hydro Network stream (2,914 km of streams). Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Ontario.
  • IoU (intersection over union): for one class, the pixels both maps call that class divided by the pixels either map does. 1 is perfect agreement.
