      How the detective works

      Each year we average the open-water season (May to October) into one number. The trend rate is Sen's slope: the middle value of the slopes between every pair of years, so one odd year cannot pull it far. The Mann-Kendall test asks whether later years beat earlier years more often than chance would allow; we call a trend significant when its p-value is below 0.05. The 95% range shows how fast or slow the true trend could plausibly be.
